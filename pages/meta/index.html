Meta

I'm a design engineer. More than anything, I love creating good digital products. I find myself doing UI, UX, front-end, back-end, systems programming and product management.

To me, a good product is fast and beautiful. It's often simple and accessible. A good product doesn't interrupt a user's flow, and a great product makes users feel like they have superpowers.

I've worked in digital agencies and startups. I was a technical co-founder at Reform. Since Reform was acquired, I've worked as a freelancer for other small startups.

If that sounds interesting, I'd love to chat.

Bjørn, the person

I'm originally from rural Denmark. After living in London and Copenhagen, I became a digital nomad. Eventually I found myself in Seoul, South Korea, and fell in love with the place. Since 2023, I've spent the majority of my time in South Korea and have occasionally spent a few months in Spain.
